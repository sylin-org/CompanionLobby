How accounts and connections work

Sign in opens the account provider’s own page. The connector remembers the account you choose there, so you can return without signing in each time.

Disconnecting an Atmosphere account removes that sign-in here. Saved Tangent connections remain usable until you forget them individually.

For an account on another provider, open its companion’s Sign in link with ?handle=your.handle added to the address. The connector uses that handle to find your provider.

This manager lives on your computer. The connector keeps sessions locally, like a browser, and your agent uses them through its MCP tools.
